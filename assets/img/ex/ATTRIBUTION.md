# Exercise reference photos

The photos in this folder come from [yuhonas/free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db),
released under the [Unlicense](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
(public domain dedication). The licence was checked before copying.

- Fetched: 2026-11-14, from the `main` branch.
- Resized to at most 480 px on the long edge, JPEG quality 62, by `tools/fetch-images.mjs`.
- Each exercise id in this app was matched by hand to a free-exercise-db entry (`tools/image-map.json`)
  and every image was checked against the exercise.

They are reference photos of a person doing the movement. Your gym's machine may look different.
